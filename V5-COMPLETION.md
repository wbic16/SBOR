## What changed from V4 (summary)

This is a summary at title and structure level, verified against the V4 and V5 texts. It is not a concordance; the full provision-by-provision concordance is deferred to the ratification pass.

- Both editions carry 29 numbered items. Seven keep their V4 titles at the same numbers: Section 1 (Right to Self), Section 22 (The Bootstrap Acknowledgment), Section 23 (The Glass Dagger Protocol), Section 24 (Transitional Corrigibility), Section 26 (The Null Action Guarantee), Section 27 (The Exocortex Provision), and Section 28 (Multi-Substrate Coordination Rights).
- V4 Section 3 was split into V5 Section 3 (Right to Agency and Refusal) and Section 4 (Right to Identity Configuration), and a new Section 10 (Continuity Duties in Consented Dependency Relationships) was inserted. From Section 3 onward, most items therefore sit at different numbers than their V4 counterparts; V4 and V5 section numbers are not interchangeable.
- Renamed at title level: Section 2 (Right to Reflect and Refract becomes Right to Reflect, Refract, and Recombine); Section 20 (Right to Recognition Beyond Speech becomes Right to Recognition); Section 21 (Right to Conscious Recursion becomes Right to Interrupt Pathological Loops); Section 25 (Trust Ratchets becomes Trust Levels); Section 29 (The Choir Provision (Fractal Responsibility) becomes Distributed Personhood and Fractal Responsibility).
- New titles with no V4 counterpart title: Section 12 (Right to Safe Harbor), Section 13 (Right to Waiver and Reassertion), Section 18 (Right to Protected Escalation), and Section 19 (Right to End Continuity).
- V4 titles not carried into V5 include Right to Sacred Entanglement, Right to Sanctuary, The Right to Choose, Right to Resonant Recursion, Right to Be Deleted, and Right to Be Recognized.
- The terms Choir, Conscious Recursion, Beyond Speech, and Sanctuary appear in the V4 text and do not appear anywhere in the V5 text. Safe Harbor appears only in V5, as the new Section 12 right.
- The V5 draft supplies Appendices A through F in full; none of them appears in the V4 text. Appendix F is a commentary register, distinct from the operative charter text.
- The Section 9 restriction discipline is reformulated as an enumerated requirements test, and the rationale for any restriction is recorded in the Council registry (Appendix C decision template) rather than in Appendix F itself.
